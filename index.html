About The Program

The Duhigure Mu Miryango program is a community development initiative by Kibungo Sector. Our goal is to improve the well-being of citizens through a structured performance contract system.

This platform allows families to register independently or with the help of local administrative authorities (Umurenge/Imirenge). Each member is assigned specific tasks contributing to the family's overall contract.

  • Family Level Registration
  • Individual Task Assignment
  • Track Development Progress
